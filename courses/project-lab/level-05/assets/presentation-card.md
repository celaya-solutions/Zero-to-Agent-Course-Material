Keep the eight-item board open. The speaker notes below are a shape, not a script that supplies missing proof. Fill with your own safe evidence. A saved or authored example stays labeled each time its result is shown.

| Clock | Say and show |
| --- | --- |
| 0:00-0:30 | This helps [role] do [job]. My evidence supports [one small claim]. My route is [live/saved/authored/mixed]. |
| 0:30-1:30 | I expected [result] from [input]. Here is the actual reply/decision and its receipt. This part is [label]. |
| 1:30-2:15 | Before, [miss or unmet need]. I changed [one rule]. With the same input, [actual after]. My verdict is [improved/unchanged/worse]. |
| 2:15-2:45 | This boundary limits [data/action]. I used [control/fallback] and observed [result]. It cannot prove [limit]. |
| 2:45-3:00 | My estimated monthly floor is [currency/value], with [largest assumption]. My next safe check is [one action]. |

Do not promise savings, customers, prices, deployments, or general safety unless your evidence supports the claim. Describe the job in everyday words. Avoid AI, agent, and model in the opening outcome sentence. Technical terms may be used later when they explain a boundary.

## Partner timing - ten minutes total

Presenter A: three-minute talk, one minute of feedback. Presenter B: three-minute talk, one minute of feedback. Use the last two minutes to make one adjustment each. Partner-read or recorded routes use the same timing. The learner still chooses evidence and answers the checks. Without a partner, record or time yourself and use the checklist.

Feedback: one claim that is supported, one missing or unclear item, and one question about the stop. Use the shared rubric. Do not score confidence, accent, speaking speed, or visual polish.

## During the final round

Three minutes speaking plus one minute transition gives six presenters in 24 minutes. The instructor uses the last minute to collect boards. More than six learners need parallel tables or a separately scheduled round; no learner loses their presentation because the class is large.

If generation takes more than a few seconds, show the saved receipt with its label and continue. If a slide or projector fails, use the worksheet. If you prefer not to speak aloud, a partner can read while you control the evidence, or use a recording and answer in writing. Public recording or publication requires separate permission.
