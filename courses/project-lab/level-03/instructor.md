## Outcome

Run a local model with external network links disconnected, compare two models using the same prompts, verify one answer, and test a named model with a missing-information rule.

## Alignment

| Target | Practice | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Distinguish local location from accuracy | Observed disconnect and response | Human observation plus local receipt; limitations stated |
| Compare fairly | Same three prompts, two models | Six identity/size/time/quality rows |
| Verify instead of trust | Open supplied public source and calculate total | Supported claim and independent arithmetic |
| Revise one rule | Named helper, before, edit, same two tests after | Normal and missing-fact verdicts, preserved failures |

## Teaching stance

Every learner needs their own Ollama-capable computer for independent practice. Support partner/saved participation without claiming their device was tested. Read commands aloud and locate the root folder together. A slow machine is a recorded constraint, not a learner failure. Do not describe named instructions as fine-tuning or a privacy guarantee.

## Prepare before learners arrive

Follow the [preparation card](preparation.md) on a fresh learner setup. Test both models on representative Windows and Mac hardware before promising class readiness. Record OS, RAM, Ollama version, exact digest/bytes, timing, and output. Downloads and dependency setup happen before the 90-minute meeting. The 8/16 GB planning targets are provisional, not measured minimums.

Print the worksheet, benchmark card, and [saved packet](assets/fallback-benchmark.md). The saved answers are authored examples with invented timings, not measured runs. Keep a local copy of the public source. Test create/check and the one-line edit. Review [verification](../../../release/level-03-verification.md) for checks actually completed and open pilots.

## Safety boundary

Only supplied public/synthetic prompts. Inspect the listener, keep loopback, close other course model work, and never disconnect a shared/remote/assistive access link. Wifi-off alone misses Ethernet/tethering. Local inference says nothing about other apps' traffic or later handling of saved files. Do not copy learner `.zta/`, inspect keys, publish a custom model, or erase receipts.

## Facilitation plan

Start with a prediction before every reveal. Demonstrate the exact `zta` commands. Point out that doctor checks availability and the receipt leaves network observation blank. Ask the partner what they actually saw. Restore links before assistant editing or upload.

For the benchmark, show the timer meaning: total request seconds including loading, with course models unloaded between requests. No stopwatch-to-first-word claim. Give 18 minutes, then switch incomplete rows to labeled saved examples. Do not cherry-pick outputs or grade fluency without evidence.

During the edit, confirm both original checks are saved. Change exactly the writing rule, create again, run the same two tests, and count words/sentences by reading the actual response. If the missing-fact answer invents a number, show the failure. A second rule attempt is allowed if time remains, with its own receipt.

## Passing proof

An observed offline answer or a labeled fallback; six benchmark rows with model identity, size, total time, and your quality verdict; one checked source; a named model tested before and after one rule edit; and a written local-versus-cloud choice.

## Grading guide

Use [the local answer key](answer-key.md). Require every proof item, with the route identified. Saved/partner work can meet the reasoning rubric; mark device/offline demonstrations Not performed where absent. Request revision for fabricated observations, blank source checks, omitted failed rows, or an unsupported invented price treated as a pass. A model failure can still be useful proof if the learner identifies and handles it honestly.

## Access and support

Provide large text, keyboard access, read-aloud prompts, and extra preparation time. Partner roles: operator, network observer, and source checker; rotate where possible and keep individual worksheets. No paid model account is required. Manual editing remains equivalent to an assistant edit.

## Fallbacks

After two attempts or five minutes on one setup fault, use the saved packet. Keep successful live rows alongside explicitly saved rows. Never substitute a connected run for an offline observation. For a partial failure, leave missing rows Not performed and explain the next exact check. An unavailable portal means saved local proof and Not submitted, not a fake upload receipt.

## After class

Confirm links restored and course requests stopped, without stopping unrelated services. Learners retain their private rules and receipts. Reopen the actual Level 3 submission in a student pilot before claiming the upload path works. Record fresh Windows/Mac, assistant-route, and observed-disconnect pilots in the verification record when they happen.
