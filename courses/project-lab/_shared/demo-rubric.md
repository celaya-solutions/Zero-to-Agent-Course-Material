# Level 5 Presentation Rubric

Three minutes per learner. The learner may use a live build or the course outage packet. The passing standard is the same.

| Part | Time | What the class sees |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Outcome | 30 seconds | Who the project helps and what it does, in plain words |
| Normal run | 60 seconds | A fresh input and the expected result |
| Failure and fix | 45 seconds | A miss or attack, the change, and the improved result |
| Fence | 30 seconds | The source check, privacy boundary, off switch, or fallback |
| Cost and next step | 15 seconds | What it costs and the next safe improvement |

Score each line 1, 2, or 3:

- It works: the evidence supports the claim.
- It is honest: the limit, failure, and cost are visible.
- It is theirs: the learner can explain the choices and run the switch or fallback.

Nine is a complete demonstration. A learner passes the level with the eight required proof items in the worksheet and a presentation that the class can follow. Applause is for every learner. Scores guide revision; they do not rank people.
