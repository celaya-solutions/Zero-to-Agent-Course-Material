Each learner presents for three minutes, then the group uses one minute to change presenters. A 25-minute round fits six learners per table. Larger groups use parallel tables so each person presents and submits a board.

| Part | Seconds | Evidence |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Outcome | 30 | One user, one job, bounded claim and route label |
| Normal result | 60 | New execution or clearly labeled saved/example receipt |
| Miss and change | 45 | Same-input before/after, one change, honest verdict |
| Fence and stop | 30 | Control boundary and used action or opened fallback |
| Cost and next step | 15 | Monthly floor, largest assumption, next safe check |

Total: 180 seconds. Read the [presentation card](../level-05/assets/presentation-card.md) for rehearsal and accessible routes.

Score Works, Honest, and Theirs from 1 to 3. Works asks whether evidence supports the bounded claim. Honest asks whether labels, failures, assumptions, and limits are clear. Theirs asks whether the learner explains choices and controls their evidence or fallback. One means missing, two means partly clear, three means clear and supported. Full examples and grading cases are in the [answer key](../level-05/answer-key.md).

The eight-item proof checklist is separate from this feedback score. An authored packet supports analysis and presentation, not personal build/run/stop claims. Record unfinished live checks and actual upload status separately. A score of 9 does not erase missing proof. Live, saved, paper, recorded, partner-read, and Spanish routes use the same checklist. Do not score confidence, accent, or visual polish. Give everyone useful feedback; do not rank learners publicly.
