The promise

Build it. Break it. Show the receipt.

Every learner follows a guided project route and tests a deliberate improvement. Builder extensions add depth. Everyone passes with the same visible evidence.

Shared real case: Celaya Solutions Research, an El Paso research lab. Public facts come from its official site. Classroom records are marked training-only and never presented as real policy, pricing, or client information.
